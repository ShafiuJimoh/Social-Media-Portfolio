Writing

Video scripts & creative concepts

Every script follows the same three‑beat structure — a hook that states the payoff in the first two seconds, a build that delivers one idea, and a single natural call to action.

Gaming · Highlight reel 15–20s

"3 Plays You Missed"

Hook

Open on the single best clip of the week, freeze‑framed — "You missed this if you weren't online Tuesday."

Build

Cut through two more plays with on‑screen numbering (1, 2, 3), fastest clip last for pacing.

CTA

"Which one was cleanest — 1, 2 or 3? Comment below."

Lifestyle · GRWM 20–25s

"Get Ready, Go Live"

Hook

Show the finished stream/content setup first — "Come with me while I get set up for tonight's upload."

Build

Three quick labelled steps: lighting check, mic test, thumbnail pick — one sentence each.

CTA

"Which thumbnail should I use — A or B?"

Entertainment · Myth vs reality 20–30s

"What People Get Wrong"

Hook

"One thing people assume about [the game/niche] that isn't true."

Build

Correct the misconception with one specific, demonstrated example — show, don't just tell.

CTA

"What myth should I bust next? Drop it below."

Gaming · Tutorial 15–20s

"The Setting You're Sleeping On"

Hook

"If your sensitivity still feels off, check this one setting first."

Build

Walk through the exact menu path on screen with numbered callouts, then show the in‑game result.

CTA

"Save this for your next match — did it help? Let me know."

Community · Reply format 15–30s

"You Asked, I Answered"

Hook

Show the actual comment on screen — "You asked how I edit my highlight clips…"

Build

Answer the real question clearly, demonstrating the process live where possible.

CTA

"Drop your next question — I'll pick one for part two."

Entertainment · Trend remix 10–15s

"Trend, But Make It Niche"

Hook

Lead with the trending sound/format's first beat, adapted instantly to the page's niche.

Build

Keep the trend's structure but swap in a niche‑specific punchline or visual payoff.

CTA

"Follow for the next one — we're not done with this trend yet."

Strategy

Sample social media growth strategy

The repeatable framework behind the calendar above — audit, pillars, a video formula, timing tests and a weekly measurement loop that replaces guesses with evidence.

01

Audit & positioning

Fix the profile first: a display name that states the niche, a legible bio with one clear call to action, and a close‑up, high‑contrast profile photo. A new visitor should understand the page's promise in under three seconds.

02

Content pillar system

Split output across 3–4 repeatable pillars (for example 40% anchor content, 25% trend‑driven, 20% behind‑the‑scenes, 15% community) so the account stays recognisable while still testing new formats.

03

The hook‑build‑CTA formula

Open on the payoff in the first two seconds, deliver exactly one idea with visible progress, then close with a single natural action — a choice, a question, or a reason to save. One ask per caption, never several.

04

Cadence & timing tests

Post on a sustainable schedule (five times a week is a realistic test volume) at two or three provisional time slots, then replace them with the platform's own "most active times" data once it's available — never guess twice.

05

Discoverability

Say the topic naturally in speech, on‑screen text and caption; use a small set of accurate, descriptive hashtags instead of unrelated reach tags; adapt trends only when they genuinely fit the niche.

06

Measure, learn, adjust

Track views, retention, and engagement per 1,000 views — (likes + comments + shares) ÷ views × 1,000 — every week. Compare medians, not single viral outliers, and change one variable at a time so results stay interpretable.
